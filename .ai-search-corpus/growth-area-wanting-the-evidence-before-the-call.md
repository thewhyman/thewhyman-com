# Growth area — Wanting the evidence before the call

tags: growth areas, weaknesses, development, blind spots, self-awareness, Wanting the evidence before the call

HOW THIS MUST BE FRAMED: CRITICAL. Every area is a TENDENCY he leans toward in specific situations — never an absence of a capability. Say 'leans toward', 'defaults to', 'his instinct is'. NEVER say he does not do something, cannot do something, struggles with something, or avoids something. NEVER an interpersonal deficit — not communication, not collaboration, not teamwork, not people skills. NEVER anything that contradicts a documented credential: he managed for 17 years, 50 engineers at Trellis and five direct reports each leading their own pods at Google, so any answer implying he does not delegate or cannot work through others is FALSE and invites 'then how did he manage for a decade?'

ANSWER SHAPE: FIVE parts, in order: (1) the STRENGTH underneath, (2) how it OVER-APPLIES as a TENDENCY, (3) the EARLY SIGNAL he uses to catch it fast, (4) the GUARD he put in place, (5) what he does INSTEAD now. Parts 3-5 are the point: anyone can name a flaw, he built a system around his.
area: Wanting the evidence before the call
strengthUnderneath: He will not assert what he has not measured, and he will kill his own work on the data. Two explorations closed at AI Fund on demand evidence. 50 artifacts seeded with known flaws, four review conditions, 240 runs, hypothesis preregistered before a single run so he could not move the goalposts.
howItOverApplies: He leans toward holding a decision open for one more measurement. Most of the time that is why the decision is right. Occasionally the window closes while the evidence is still being gathered, and a good-enough call made on Tuesday would have beaten a correct one made on Friday.
earlySignal: He watches for the moment he adds a condition to an experiment that would not change the decision either way. That is rigor turning into deferral.
guard: He preregisters the decision rule alongside the hypothesis. If a result would not change the call, the run does not happen.
insteadNow: He ships the smallest testable unit and lets the gates catch what is wrong, rather than pre-verifying everything up front. Evals fixed before the build time-box the rigor instead of letting it expand.
