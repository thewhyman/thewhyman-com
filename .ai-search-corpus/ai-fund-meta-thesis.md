# AI Fund — the meta thesis

tags: ai fund, andrew ng, lessons, product judgment

You cannot reason your way to the destination. You start with a hypothesis, test it with real users, pivot on what you learn, and start again with a sharper hypothesis. There is no other loop. Every one of the nine lessons only became real by running that loop compressed. That is what a studio buys you — not the destination, but the loop. (Credited to Eli Chen, Technology Partner at AI Fund.)
