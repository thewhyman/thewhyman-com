# How Anand leads — style, culture and the bar he sets

tags: leadership, management style, culture, how he leads, team, postmortem

culture: No-blame postmortems, and engineers who own outcomes rather than tickets. He has run this at Google across a $40B portfolio and at Trellis across 50 engineers.
scope: Peaked at 16 reports at Google (5 direct, each leading a pod) across a ~$40B portfolio. 6 senior architects and 50+ engineers influenced at Trellis. A 15-person core team at 21CT.
howHeLeads: Sets technical direction and writes the code himself, so reviews are grounded rather than delegated. Owns P&L on multi-million-dollar vendor contracts and runs build-vs-buy analysis rather than accepting a vendor demo.
disagreement: He will take a technical disagreement to senior VPs with the case built, and has accepted personal cost for it — he was removed from a project after successfully arguing the business's preferred vendor carried the same flaw that killed the predecessor system. The system shipped and returned $51M over five years.
