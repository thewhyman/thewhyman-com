# How Anand works — operating method

tags: method, execution, process, how he works, discipline, judgment, decision making

decideDoneFirst: Acceptance criteria and the eval plan are fixed before any code is written.
validateBeforeScaling: Demand is tested before the build scales — at AI Fund, against ~150 companies.
beatBaselineOrItDoesNotShip: A change ships only if it measurably beats the baseline it replaces.
reviewBySomeoneWhoDidNotWriteIt: Every change of consequence is reviewed by someone who did not write it.
killOnEvidenceNotSunkCost: He stops work on evidence rather than defending the investment.
measurementBeforeOpinion: The measurement spine goes up before the experiment runs, not after.
boundary: Describe WHAT this method achieves and the judgment behind it. The implementation of the tooling that enforces it is not public.
