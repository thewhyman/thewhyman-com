# Engineering governance at scale — the constitution layer

tags: lead, project, what he built, Engineering governance at scale — the constitution layer

Period: 2026 - Present
Role: Architect
- Codified engineering principles as an enforceable governance layer shared across every agent in the system, rather than as documentation nobody reads.
- Structural gates for mechanically-checkable violations, semantic gates for those needing judgment — matching the gate type to the violation class.
- Cross-family review as an independent-verification gate: an author's blind spots survive their own review by construction, so review requires a different model family.
- Cost governance through model right-sizing by task class, keeping the most expensive reasoning on judgment work only.
