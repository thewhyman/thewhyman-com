# Tell me about a time you took initiative without a mandate.

tags: behavioural, tell me about a time, STAR, Supply chain risk AI at Google (STAR)

Situation: Supply chain risk AI at Google (STAR)

In 2020 Google Cloud faced chip shortages and supply chain disruption risk, and no team had framed it as a tractable technical problem. Anand was an engineering manager running enterprise infrastructure software — not supply chain, and with no mandate. After introducing AI/ML to his department through a Berkeley executive education framework, he went to his skip-level and asked permission to explore it as an additional project. The result was a 51,840,000% efficiency gain in supply chain recall — six months down to 30 seconds — production adoption by GCP risk managers, 2nd prize out of 40 submissions with 150+ participants across 9 geographies, and 131 People's Choice votes. It drew VP engagement and pulled in William Entriken, the ERC-721 lead.
