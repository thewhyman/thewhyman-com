# Growth area — Opening a second frontier

tags: growth areas, weaknesses, development, blind spots, self-awareness, Opening a second frontier

HOW THIS MUST BE FRAMED: CRITICAL. Every area is a TENDENCY he leans toward in specific situations — never an absence of a capability. Say 'leans toward', 'defaults to', 'his instinct is'. NEVER say he does not do something, cannot do something, struggles with something, or avoids something. NEVER an interpersonal deficit — not communication, not collaboration, not teamwork, not people skills. NEVER anything that contradicts a documented credential: he managed for 17 years, 50 engineers at Trellis and five direct reports each leading their own pods at Google, so any answer implying he does not delegate or cannot work through others is FALSE and invites 'then how did he manage for a decade?'

ANSWER SHAPE: FIVE parts, in order: (1) the STRENGTH underneath, (2) how it OVER-APPLIES as a TENDENCY, (3) the EARLY SIGNAL he uses to catch it fast, (4) the GUARD he put in place, (5) what he does INSTEAD now. Parts 3-5 are the point: anyone can name a flaw, he built a system around his.
area: Opening a second frontier
strengthUnderneath: He sees a tractable problem nobody has framed yet and moves on it. Running enterprise infrastructure software at Google with no supply-chain mandate, he went to his skip-level, asked permission to explore supply chain risk as an additional project, and took recall from six months to thirty seconds.
howItOverApplies: His instinct when he spots that opening is to take it while the first thing is still running. gPals was 14 prototype iterations and 50+ in-person customer interviews, alongside the day job. The work compounds, but the attention is split, and the people depending on the first frontier feel it before he does.
earlySignal: The tell is when he stops being the person others are waiting on and starts being the person others are waiting for. If a decision on the primary track is sitting because he is deep in the second one, the split has gone too far.
guard: He asks permission explicitly and scopes it as additional rather than instead — which is what he did with the skip-level on supply chain, and why that one worked. Unscoped exploration is the failure mode; sanctioned exploration is the strength.
insteadNow: He fixes the acceptance criteria for both tracks before starting the second, so the primary has a defined bar he can be held to rather than an implicit one he might drift from.
