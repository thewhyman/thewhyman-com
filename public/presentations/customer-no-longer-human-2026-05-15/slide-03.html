02 · The Shift
03 / 07

Attention is being replaced by execution.

The internet's primary metric stops being impressions and becomes outcomes.

What gets less valuable
Old loop
attention › click › impression › conversion
  • Creative built to hijack attention
  • SEO tuned for human reading
  • Funnel and dopamine UX
  • Generic content at infinite scale
What becomes scarce
New loop
intent › delegation › execution › outcome
  • Taste, judgment, and curation
  • Verifiable truth and provenance
  • Proprietary data and real-world signal
  • In-real-life experience, human time
